# Developer distribution plan

Distribution should prove that qualified teams can evaluate and integrate the
product, not merely increase stars.

## Ideal users

1. POS, ordering, payment, and restaurant software platforms adding loyalty.
2. Multi-unit restaurant technical teams that need data ownership and portable
   checkout/refund behavior.
3. Restaurant integrators and agencies repeatedly rebuilding loyalty plumbing.

The repository is not currently optimized for operators seeking a turnkey
no-code service without a technical owner.

## Twelve-week motion

- Weeks 1-2: publish the platform walkthrough, quickstart, architecture, wallet,
  Square, security, and migration pages; create one reproducible launch release.
- Weeks 3-4: recruit ten problem interviews through founder-led LinkedIn and
  relevant engineering communities. Link to the walkthrough, not a sales form.
- Weeks 5-8: turn repeated questions into searchable technical guides and public
  issues; help three independent users reach their first sandbox evaluation.
- Weeks 9-12: select up to two design partners with a real ordering/refund edge,
  produce sanitized adapter evidence, and publish only approved results.

## Channel contract

- GitHub: release notes, good-first issues, integration requests, Discussions,
  and reproducible reports.
- LinkedIn: founder/operator lessons with one artifact and one CTA per post.
- SEO: original pages answering technical purchase and implementation questions;
  no keyword-stuffed comparison claims.
- GEO: keep `llms.txt`, OpenAPI, schemas, boundaries, examples, and evidence easy
  for agents to retrieve; do not treat AI indexes as ranking guarantees.

Use [metrics.md](metrics.md) for funnel definitions. Repository traffic, clones,
stars, and npm downloads are signals; qualified walkthrough completion,
sandbox evaluation, refund-safe integration, and active pilots are outcomes.
