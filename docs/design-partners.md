# Design partner program

LIP is looking for ordering platforms, restaurant technology teams, and
multi-location operators that need a portable loyalty transaction layer.

## Good fit

- You control a server-side ordering or checkout integration.
- Refund, void, retry, offline, modifier, or split-tender behavior matters.
- You can provide a test tenant and sanitized event examples.
- An engineer and product owner can meet weekly during the integration.

## Six-week pilot shape

1. Map the current order, member, balance, and refund semantics.
2. Certify a source adapter against the foodservice fixture corpus.
3. Run evaluate through partial refund in staging with deterministic keys.
4. Rehearse member/balance migration and reconcile the checksummed result.
5. Document security, operations, cutover, rollback, and ownership.
6. Decide whether to self-host, use Cloud, or stop without lock-in.

## Evidence and privacy

Success means a reproducible lifecycle report, not a logo or testimonial. LIP
will not publish a partner name, metric, screenshot, or quote without written
approval. Sanitized examples must exclude customer PII and credentials.
