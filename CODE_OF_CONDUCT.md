# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free project for everyone. Be
respectful, discuss ideas rather than people, assume good intent while still
addressing harm, and make technical disagreement specific and reproducible.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, discriminatory language,
unwanted sexual attention, publishing private information, and sustained
disruption of project spaces.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to `opensource@craveup.com`. Maintainers may
edit or remove contributions, issue a warning, temporarily restrict
participation, or permanently ban a participant. Reports are handled as
confidentially as practical; conflicts of interest are routed to another
maintainer. This policy applies in repository, community, and project event
spaces and when someone represents the project publicly.

This policy is adapted from the Contributor Covenant 2.1.
